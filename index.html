About

The long way to the booth.

NEXUS is the stage name of a selector raised on late-night radio, warehouse raves, and the drive home when the last record still echoes. For more than a decade the booth has been an instrument — reading rooms, stretching breakdowns, and turning packed floors into one shared pulse.

    Bedroom frequencies

    First all-nighters with a pair of second-hand CDJs, crate-digging vinyl, and mixes recorded straight to a cheap recorder.

  2. 2016

    Warehouse resident

    Weekly 3am slots at Warehouse 14. Learned how to hold a room when the lights stay low and the floor is still filling.

  3. 2018

    Electric Horizon

    First festival sunset set. Twelve thousand people, one borrowed USB, and a closer that still gets requested.

  4. 2020

    Afterglow EP

    Debut four-track EP crosses two million streams. The title cut becomes a peak-time staple on late radio.

  5. 2022

    Circuit cities

    Berlin, Ibiza, Mumbai. Club residencies stacked between red-eyes, plus a Breakthrough DJ award.

  6. 2024–26

    Headliner era

    Neon Pulse Festival headline. New album Voltage in motion. Still chasing the perfect last-hour blend.
